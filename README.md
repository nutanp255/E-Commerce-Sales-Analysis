# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Python to understand sales performance, profitability, product trends, and regional performance. The analysis explores the dataset, prepares data for analysis, performs exploratory and statistical analysis, and presents findings through visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze sales, profit, and quantity.
- Identify the top 10 products by sales.
- Understand the sales share of each product category.
- Explore the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Examine the distribution of profit and identify transactions with losses.
- Study correlations between numerical variables.
- Use sales insights to support business decisions.

## 📂 Dataset Description
- **Dataset Name:** Sample Superstore Dataset
- **File Format:** CSV (Comma-Separated Values)
- **Number of Records (Rows):** 9,994
- **Number of Columns:** 21

[View Dataset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leonism/sample-superstore/refs/heads/master/data/superstore.csv)

### Key Attributes
- **Order ID:** Identifies an order.
- **Order Date:** Date the order was placed.
- **Customer Segment:** Customer classification.
- **Region:** Geographical sales region.
- **Category and Sub-Category:** Product classifications.
- **Sales:** Sales amount.
- **Quantity:** Number of units sold.
- **Discount:** Discount applied to an order item.
- **Profit:** Profit or loss generated.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies
- **Python** — data analysis and manipulation
- **Pandas** — data loading, cleaning, and analysis
- **NumPy** — numerical operations
- **Matplotlib** — charts and graphs
- **Seaborn** — statistical visualizations
- **Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab** — writing and running the analysis

## 🧹 Data Preparation and Understanding
The notebook includes data loading and understanding, identification of numerical and categorical columns, missing-value checks, and data cleaning before analysis. Numerical and categorical data are considered separately when preparing the dataset.

## 🔎 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Exploratory Data Analysis is used to inspect the dataset and find patterns in sales, profit, products, categories, discounts, and other relevant variables.

## 📈 Statistical Analysis
Statistical analysis is used to summarize numerical data and investigate relationships among variables. Correlation analysis helps show how numerical variables are related.

## 📊 Data Visualization
Matplotlib and Seaborn are used to communicate findings through charts and graphs, making patterns and comparisons easier to understand. The notebook includes:
- A bar chart comparing total sales, profit, and quantity
- A horizontal bar chart showing the top 10 products by sales
- A chart showing each category's share of sales
- A scatter plot exploring the relationship between discounts and profit
- A histogram showing the distribution of profit
- A correlation heatmap for numerical variables

## 💡 Key Findings and Business Insights
1. **Sales Performance:** Identify the products and categories generating the highest sales.
2. **Product Performance:** The top 10 products by sales highlight products that contribute significantly to revenue.
3. **Category Analysis:** Technology has the highest sales share in the notebook's analysis, followed by Furniture, Office Supplies, and Clothing.
4. **Discount Strategy:** The scatter plot indicates a negative relationship between discounts and profit; higher discounts generally correspond to lower profit.
5. **Profit Distribution:** Most transactions have low to moderate profits, while some transactions result in losses and a few generate high profits.
6. **Correlation Analysis:** The heatmap helps identify positive and negative relationships between numerical variables.
7. **Business Planning:** Sales and regional performance analysis can help businesses plan inventory, review sales strategies, and identify areas for improvement.

## ✅ Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Python libraries can be used to analyze the Sample Superstore Dataset and communicate findings with visualizations. The analysis covers sales and profit performance, top products, category-level sales share, discount patterns, profit distribution, and correlations between numerical variables. These insights can help a business review its discount strategy, focus on strong-performing products and categories, reduce losses, and make more informed, data-driven decisions.
